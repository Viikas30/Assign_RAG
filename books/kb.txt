pricing details for autostream plans and their features 
PLAN: Basic
Price: $29 per month
Video Limit: 10 videos per month
Maximum Resolution: 720p
Additional Features: None

PLAN: Pro
Price: $79 per month
Video Limit: Unlimited videos
Maximum Resolution: 4K
Additional Features:
- AI-generated captions
- 24/7 customer support

COMPANY POLICIES

Refund Policy:
- Refunds are not available after 7 days from purchase.

Customer Support Policy:
- 24/7 support is available only for Pro plan subscribers.
- Basic plan does not include 24/7 support.
